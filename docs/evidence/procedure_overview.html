The procedure, stage by stage

  1. 1
    Corpus✓ done

    DBNL works with TEI structure (on the 2 TB disk). The current sample: 39 works, 44,372 food-word occurrences.

    OCR cookbooks without TEI and Delpher newspapers are outside this procedure until their structure is read.

    plans/steps/DELPHER_KRANTEN.md

  2. 2
    Structure: entries and headings? open question

    TEI entries and headings are read (segmenter shipped); a recipe or remedy inherits its heading's purpose.

    The heading cue is narrow and unvalidated. Frames live at two levels: the heading gives the purpose, the sentence the procedure.

    plans/steps/TRIANGULATION.md

  3. 3
    Finding passages: two routes● in progress

    Food route: every food word from the vocabulary. Verb route: a food word with a reviewed verb nearby (321 lemmas, 1,771 forms, three review rounds).

    Which route per genre. First result: recipe books need no verb filter; literary works do; medical books are unclear.

    plans/steps/VERB_LEXICON.md

  4. 4
    Cheap filter before the language model✓ done

    A small trained model (GloBERTise) drops clear non-food passages at threshold 0.98; saves about 43% of calls outside recipe and medical books.

    PLAN.md

The common thread

Different fixes for Step B and Step C failed in the same way. What they shared is the unit: one label per sentence-sized window. Frames often live a level higher, in the heading (71% of 3,326 recipes name their goal only in the title). The two-level design (heading gives purpose, sentence gives procedure) is the answer under test; the genre question decides where each level and route applies.
